How the score works

TF-IDF over unigrams and bigrams, fit on the KSA catalog (and separately on the task catalog), compared by cosine similarity. Acronyms are expanded on both sides first, so "IDS" also scores as "intrusion detection system". A score of 1.00 means identical wording; real matches land between 0.15 and 0.50. Strong is 0.30 or higher, moderate is 0.18 or higher. Each match quotes the sentence of your text that scores highest against it, with the shared words marked.
